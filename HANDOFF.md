- Map LOD collision uses estimated text boxes, not getBBox (the Trip page is display:none at init), so a near-miss can still touch; shields are hand-placed mid-leg over the route (no mockup existed for them).
- A seeded charge or expense keeps its day/stopId if departDate changes later. Charges take stopId from the nearest stop by distance (Day 1 sessions → "start"); expenses take the stop of their day like manual entries.
